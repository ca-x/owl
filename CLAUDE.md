## Design Context

### Users

Owl serves bilingual readers who look up words repeatedly, people who maintain MDX and MDD libraries, and readers who use the desktop app offline or with a self-hosted server.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, bookish. Give the headword, the definition and its dictionary source the most prominent places on the page.

### Aesthetic Direction

The default daylight interface takes its cues from a printed dictionary. Use warm paper, ink, restrained oxblood accents and serif headings. System dark mode follows the operating system. The named electronic-device themes have their own palettes and materials.

### Design Principles

- Keep typing, selecting a suggestion and reading its entry within one keyboard flow. Preserve input-method composition and browser history.
- Use the same content edges in the header, search form, entry and library. Keep controls finger-sized on narrow screens and allow bilingual labels to wrap.
- Reserve stronger shadows for temporary surfaces. Respect reduced motion, system focus conventions and the reader's font choice.
- Open dictionary management on the library. Present personal preferences, MCP access and site administration under their own controls.
- Keep local and server sources identifiable in the native client. Local reading works offline.
